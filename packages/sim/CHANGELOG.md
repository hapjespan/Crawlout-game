# @crawlout/sim

- 0.38.2 (audit fixes, round 2; data, protocol and STATE_FORMAT_VERSION 16 unchanged). (1) `territoryOf` (systems/placement.part1.ts) caches the mask per player on its source list (compared element-wise) instead of `structVersion * 65536 + sources`: an owner change without alloc or free (a project part reverted and another restored in one tick) gave the same key and a stale mask, while a freshly resynced client computes the right one. (2) `deserializeState` (state.ts `validateLoaded`) checks the allocator after loading (World.alloc and free are its only writers): alive 0 or 1, a live entity has a generation, the free stack holds each free index below highWater once, live plus free equals highWater; and an active flow slot targets a rectangle inside the map. (3) `spawnCamp` (systems/creeps.part1.ts): a full world stops the spawn (break, not return) and the camp keeps who spawned; with nobody placed it stays cleared (the respawn timer retries) instead of stacking a set each tick or going idle without bandits. (4) Raider harass (systems/aiRaiders.part2.ts) no longer re-sends a buggy that guardPosts gave Guard that tick, nor re-orders a buggy already on its AttackMove to the spot or a hurt one already on its Move home. (5) `richBuild` of the Raider AI is the one in ai.plans.ts (the copy in aiRaiders.part1.ts removed). (6) Survival wave units: `nearestEnemy` (systems/survival.part2.ts) tests the distance before `isEnemy` (same result, both pure), a 216-unit stress went from 1.56 to about 1.43 ms per tick; no hash moves. The audit's throttle (a scan every 8 ticks per unit) was tried and dropped: it re-recorded s17 but the duo test then loses slot 1's HQ at frame 5126 (wave units keep walking at the HQ instead of turning on its defenders), a balance change. Not changed: the Dweller rally (ai.think.ts) still re-issues AttackMove to units on their way to an own claim point (raidOver counts them idle); the Raider guard there moves 14 fixtures and breaks the tuned seeds of match-length, mirror and raiders-balance, so it needs a balance pass. Tests: test/audit-fixes-2.test.ts.
- 0.38.1 (audit fixes; data, protocol and STATE_FORMAT_VERSION 16 unchanged). (1) `applyLoad` (systems/garde.transport.ts) gives the order through `replaceOrder` (now exported from systems/commands.part1.ts), so a unit in a paid retraining that is told to board gets its scrap back like with every other order (`refundCraft`). Carried scrap follows replaceOrder: it stays on the unit while it walks to the transport; a passenger slot has no carry, so what it still carries is lost when it boards (open: bank it or add a cargo field, a state change). (2) `craft` (systems/orders.part2.ts) rechecks the workshop while a settler waits for scrap (PHASE_WORK): with no reachable workshop left it goes back to PHASE_GO, so it no longer pays and retrains at a razed one (the case economy.ts needsBuilder describes). Not an interactRange check: separation pushes waiting settlers up to about 100 units out of it while their workshop stands, and that variant shifted s4, s6 and s10 and the Raiders balance test. Open: a razed workshop with another one elsewhere still lets the settler pay where it stands (needs the order to remember its workshop, a state change). (3) `heroSystem` (systems/hero.ts): a revive that finds the world full (createHero fails) takes its `heroRevives` back, instead of counting a revive every tick; the count still goes up before createHero, which scales the revived hero with it. (4) Housing growth (systems/economy.ts) adds nothing to the census when createUnit fails and adds the popCost of the type it spawned (the builder of the safety net), as safetyNet.ts does. (5) Dead code removed: `isRuin` (systems/project.ts) and `fnv1aString` (math/fnv.ts). Not changed: `combat.ts` lastHit; `isEnemy` refuses a target at hp 0, so no shot after the killing one lands in the same tick and plunder already goes to the killer (pinned by a test). Tests: test/audit-fixes.test.ts. No fixture shifts; nothing re-recorded.

- 0.34.0 (frames need no bed; data 0.41.0, protocol 0.18.0 unchanged, STATE_FORMAT_VERSION 16 unchanged; Martien: "de garde heeft geen opties om bedden te maken hoe zit dit?"). Cause: `systems/census.ts` counted every unit that was not a robot as a settler in a bed and `applyProduce` (systems/robots.ts) asked a free bed for every non-robot, so a krachtpantser or plasmaridder (3 pop) and the Vertibird (4) took beds and could not be produced with full beds; the Garde start (6 units in the 6 Bastion beds) left nothing for growth or frames. Now only bodyType human sleeps in a bed (data/factions.json): frames, vehicles and beasts (also the Raider buggy and dog) go into `Census.noBedPop`, count for popCapMax (popCap, totalPop) only, and a militia one still counts as a fighter. `PlayerStatus.robotPop` now reports robots plus that no-bed pop, so pop + robotPop stays within popCapMax as the protocol doc says (proposal for the architect: the field means "pop without a bed"). Housing growth (housingGrowthPerMin 1 for the Garde) works with the new Slaapzaal. Tests: test/garde-beds.test.ts (a frame with every bed taken; the Slaapzaal and growth), garde.test.ts no longer gives the frame a kazerne, garde-b.test.ts the Vertibird is no settler. All fixtures re-recorded (dataVersion and the building list and damage matrix in the envHash).
